What changed with enemy styles?

A default-only opponent selects ranged strafing for spear thrust (18/18 wins); the uniform enemy-style mix instead puts defensive first (87.5%). This shift is descriptive—the default-only baseline has just 18 games/style. The other eight selected styles agree, although pepper stream is tied between balanced and ranged strafing in the mixed pool.

Patterns

  • Aggressive closes lunge slash’s preferred distance from about 95 to 57 units; it is a large, consistent improvement in this experiment.
  • Defensive moves widening laser from a 45-unit preferred distance to 105. This is the other clear improvement; it better uses the widening beam away from its origin.
  • Defensive has the highest aggregate score (55.3%) and leads four moves, but this does not prove it is the best style for every move. Aggressive leads two, ranged strafing two, balanced one (tied).
  • These are whole-loadout outcomes with common built-ins. Ranged strafing falls back to the balanced stance for short-reach attacks, but built-in Shoot fallback can still make its policy differ on melee loadouts.
Method, limitations and downloads

Approximate two-sided paired t tests on 72 condition-seed blocks. Holm correction across all 54 pairwise style comparisons (six per move). A unique winner must beat every alternative after correction. Paired block-bootstrap 95% percentile intervals, 10,000 resamples, are descriptive; ranking alone does not establish a unique best style.

Fixed nine-move pool, 11x11 Moderate gym, 25 seconds, built-ins enabled. Two seeds per specific matchup are insufficient for individual matchup conclusions. T inference is approximate for discrete scores; multiple-comparison correction does not remove model assumptions. Non-significance does not establish equivalence.

The uniform mixture estimates a generalist over this specified reference pool. A default-only enemy measures performance against that one policy and can reward exploiting it. With four enemy styles, stratifying all four avoids the extra mixture noise of sampling styles at random. Change the weights if a different opponent population matters.

Bootstrap intervals resample paired condition-seed blocks. Tests are approximate paired t tests on score differences; family-wise correction covers all 54 primary comparisons, including selection of the apparent winner. Default-enemy rankings are descriptive only.

Replay examples are selected same-opponent cases for the default and highest-scoring style, not an independent validation set. No production or calibration settings were changed.
