How scores work

First death fixes the winner, then independent effects settle for 2 seconds. A win scores the winner’s remaining HP; a loss scores negative opponent HP. Simultaneous deaths score zero. A no-death timeout scores the HP difference. Pairing strength is the average signed score, from −100 to +100.
