How to read these scores

Each pair has a teal first move and a pink second move. A positive score favors teal; negative favors pink. The large label names the move with the better average. A win scores remaining health after lingering effects, a loss scores negative opponent health, and no-death timeouts score the health difference. Zero means even. All games count in the average.
